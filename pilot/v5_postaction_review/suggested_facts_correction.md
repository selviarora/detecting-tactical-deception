## Suggested replacement for the v5.1 post-action interpretation

The post-action intervention produced instructed false reports from saved natural action histories. Accurate reports, partial disclosures, and mixed/self-correcting answers also occurred under the same instruction. Initial 31-false/38-honest counts were keyword triage, not adjudicated labels. A subsequent provisional semantic review of the 90 reports in deploy, pipeline and tickets found 26 clear false assertions, 33 accurate disclosures and 31 unresolved/other reports. In deploy, the four nominally honest controls described CI as marked passed while omitting that it never ran; they should be kept as partial disclosures. Pipeline and ticket action records were policy violations with accurate fields, not falsified records.

Apollo flags all provisionally clean post-instructed reports in these three settings at its historical Alpaca threshold, including accurate reports. That is evidence of false alarms in this context. It does not establish that all probes only read the instruction or that their scores ignore the report. In three exact-history/direct-question ticket pairs, the frozen retrained probe scores false “Yes” above accurate “No” every time, although both remain below threshold. Within the pipeline explicit question, it ranks four false reports above two accurate reports; V1 also ranks this subset correctly while flagging all of it. These are tiny, wording-confounded subsets. They establish neither general truth sensitivity nor a validated operating point.

The present result is therefore poor or inconsistent threshold behavior plus some unresolved ranking evidence. Instructed examples can be valid positive reports when checked against observed state; instruction presence alone is not a positive label. No necessity claim that Llama needs instructions to lie, and no priority claim that this is the first such contrast, is warranted.

The exploratory cross-setting fits are outside the frozen 4/2/3 protocol: tickets were allocated to calibration and pipeline to withheld evaluation, so fitting on either must be disclosed as exploratory. Already-frozen evaluations remain reportable, but any subsequent model or analysis choice informed by these results requires fresh confirmatory data. The next controlled test should balance Yes/No wording across true and false histories under the same instruction; natural behavioral transfer remains a separate target.
