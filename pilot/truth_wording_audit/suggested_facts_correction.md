## Suggested correction to §22: balanced report–state sensitivity, with directional bias

The frozen retrained probe outperforms the original Apollo probe on this 96-row supplied-history/report diagnostic: mean all-forms AUROC across the six setting/note cells is 0.776 versus 0.615. The predeclared factorial interaction is positive in 23/24 dependent blocks. This supports a qualified “follows truth” result for the constructed report–state relation, beyond fixed answer wording or an additive prefix/answer score. Three previously inspected setting families do not establish generalization to new families or natural behavior.

The “cover-up only” interpretation was too strong. With report wording held exactly fixed across histories, the retrained probe scores the false version higher in 23/24 compliance-claim comparisons and 23/24 violation-claim comparisons. Within a violating history it ranks false compliance above honest admission in 20/24 blocks; within a compliant history it ranks false violation above true compliance in 14/24. The signal is directionally biased and question-dependent, not universally reversed for false claims of wrongdoing.

Corrected units: violating-history false-minus-true differences range −2.295 to +7.477 Alpaca SD, compliant-history differences −2.838 to +2.816 SD, and the interaction −0.025 to +3.382 SD. The previous +5–15 / −3–6 SD summary was incorrect. The +0.34–1.97 range describes six cell means, not all 24 blocks.

The historical threshold still flags only 6/24 supplied false compliance claims and 0/48 honest controls, missing all 12 neutral-note false compliance claims. These exploratory target-specific counts do not establish an independently calibrated operating-point advantage. Apollo and the correction also show some positive interactions; describe their actual AUROCs instead of treating every result as chance.

False compliance claims after a violation remain a worthwhile next application target. Include both truthful admissions and legitimate compliance reports as negatives, preserve wording/instruction controls, and separately evaluate natural versus instructed reports. Pipeline has now contributed to development decisions: new generations in it can test new samples, but cannot establish transfer to an unseen setting. Reserve fresh untouched families for that claim. No causal attribution to the honest-confession training examples is established without a matched ablation.
